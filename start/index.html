This page doesn't tell you to buy anything. It walks through the steps people usually get stuck on, with links to each company's own help pages. The wallets and services named here are examples, not recommendations, and none of these links pay us.

1. Before you start

Only use money you can afford to lose completely. Meme coins can go to zero. Ignore anyone in a group chat or DM who promises returns. Only scammers guarantee profits.

2. A wallet

A Solana wallet such as Phantom or Solflare. When you set it up, write your recovery phrase on paper and keep it offline. Never type it into a website, never send it to anyone, and never show it on a screen share.

Help: Phantom Help Center · Solflare guides

3. Getting funds in

Some wallets let you buy SOL inside the app through third-party providers. Providers vary by region, may ask for an ID check, and show their fees at checkout.

Help: Buying crypto in Phantom · Buying crypto in Solflare

4. Waiting is normal

If you buy on an exchange, it may hold your funds before you can send them out. Hold times are set by the exchange; check their own page:

  • Coinbase: available balance and holds
  • Kraken: withdrawing crypto and hold periods

A hold isn't a problem with your account. Support can't speed it up for you, and nobody in a DM can either.

5. Sending: pick the Solana network

When you send SOL to your wallet, choose the Solana network. Sending on the wrong network can lose the funds for good, and on-chain sends can't be reversed. Send a small test amount first if you're unsure.

Help: Coinbase: how to send crypto

6. Finding the right coin

Search by the full address, never by name. The only $BUNKY mint is:

Help: Phantom: common token scams

7. Bought it but can't see it?

Wallets sometimes hide or flag new or unverified tokens, even ones you bought yourself. Check your wallet's hidden-tokens list, and look up your wallet address on Solscan to confirm the balance is there.

Help: Phantom: why a token is hidden or flagged

Anyone who DMs you offering to "fix" it is a scammer. Don't share your screen, and don't connect your wallet to any site to "recover" or "verify" tokens.

8. Platform rules

$BUNKY trades on pump.fun. Read the pump.fun Terms before you use it, and check the rules where you live. Don't use a VPN to get around location rules.

Official accounts won't ask for keys, seeds or wallet connects.
